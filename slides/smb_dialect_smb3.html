SMB 3.0 & 3.0.2 (Encryption & Speed)

DIALECT CODES: 0x0300 / 0x0302
SECURITY & HIGH PERFORMANCE Native Encryption
  • Native AES Encryption: Introduced end-to-end AES-128-CCM encryption without requiring IPsec.
  • SMB Multichannel & Direct: Aggregates multiple network cards and supports zero-CPU RDMA memory transfers.
  • Secure Dialect Negotiation: Protects against Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) downgrade attacks.
Cybersecurity Benefit: Eavesdropping on wire data payloads is prevented by default.
