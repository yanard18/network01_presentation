SSH vs. Telnet

SSH (Port 22) vs. Telnet (Port 23)
• Default Port: TCP 22 vs. TCP 23
• Channel Security: Fully Encrypted (AES / ChaCha20) vs. Cleartext / Unencrypted
• Data Payload: Ciphertext (Credentials & Keystrokes protected) vs. Plaintext (Visible to Wireshark sniffing)
• Packet Integrity: Cryptographic HMACs (Tamper-proof) vs. No Integrity Protection (MITM vulnerable)
• Current Status: Universal Secure Standard vs. Obsolete & High-Risk Threat
