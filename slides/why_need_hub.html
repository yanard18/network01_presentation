Why Do We Need a HUB?

2 Devices = 1 Cable
➔
5 Devices = 10 Mesh Cables!
SOLUTION Centralized Star Topology with HUB

Connecting \(N\) devices directly requires \(\frac{N(N-1)}{2}\) cables and multiple NICs per computer. A HUB solves this cabling nightmare by serving as a single central junction—allowing every computer to join the network using just 1 cable!
