IPv4 vs. IPv6

IPv4 vs. IPv6
• Address Size: 32-bit (4.3 Billion IPs) vs. 128-bit (3.4 × 10³⁸ IPs)
• Header Format: Variable (20–60B) with Checksum vs. Fixed (40B) without Checksum
• Address Assignment: Manual / DHCP & NAT vs. Stateless Auto-Config (SLAAC)
• Security: Optional IPSec vs. Built-in Native IPSec Support
